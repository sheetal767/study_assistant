# 🧠 StudyFlow AI — Multi-Agent Study Assistant

StudyFlow AI is an AI-powered app that helps you learn any subject step by step using Google Gemini. It acts like your own personal study team by breaking down complex topics into short lessons and quizzes.

🔗 **Live App:** [studyassistant-drpcf3l6c3q34ff9zjcgfk.streamlit.app](https://studyassistant-drpcf3l6c3q34ff9zjcgfk.streamlit.app/)

---

## 🌟 What This App Does

* 🗺️ **Creates Study Plans:** Enter any subject (like "DBMS") and the app divides it into easy, bite-sized lessons.
* 📖 **Explains Topics Simply:** Gives clear explanations with real-life examples.
* ✍️ **Tests Your Knowledge:** Generates short multiple-choice quizzes to check what you learned.
* 📊 **Tracks Progress:** Shows how many topics you completed and your quiz scores.
* 🌓 **Dark Mode Option:** Switch between light and dark themes anytime.
